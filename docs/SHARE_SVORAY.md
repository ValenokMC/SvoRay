## Short English post

> SvoRay is a focused Windows 10/11 client for people who already have a v2rayN-compatible
> subscription and want a simpler daily interface: paste the subscription, pick a server, press
> one button. It supports RU/EN, Xray, sing-box, TUN and system proxy, while the full v2rayN
> interface remains one click away. GPL-3.0, installer and portable ZIP available:

## Longer technical angle

Useful points for an article, forum post, or video description:

1. SvoRay is a focused interface over v2rayN 7.24.4, not a new protocol or subscription service.
2. It solves the daily workflow: import, select, connect, check, and route selected domains.
3. Advanced v2rayN controls remain available instead of being reimplemented incompletely.
4. The client ships Xray and sing-box cores, supports TUN and Windows system proxy, and stores user
   data under `%LOCALAPPDATA%\SvoRay`.
5. Russian and English resources are checked by tests. Subscription-provided support links are
   validated and open only after an explicit click.
6. Release artifacts include SHA-256 checksums and complete corresponding source under GPL-3.0.
7. The installer is not commercially signed, so SmartScreen may warn and users should verify the
   published checksum.
